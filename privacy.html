Privacy Policy

JM Heating & AC

Effective date: November 8, 2026

JM Heating & AC provides residential heating and air conditioning services in Western Pennsylvania. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and disclosed when you visit www.jmheatingac.com or contact us through the options listed on our website.

Information You Provide

Our website provides links to call, text, or email us. If you choose to contact us, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, service address, information about your heating or cooling system, and details of your request. We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, arrange appointments, prepare estimates, communicate about services, and carry out requested work.

The website does not currently offer an online contact form, customer account, or payment checkout. If those features are added, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Website Hosting and Technical Information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in to GitHub. GitHub may process additional technical information under its own privacy practices. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts and External Websites

This website uses an HTML5 UP design template and loads a font through Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font from Google's servers, Google receives technical information needed to deliver the font, including your IP address. For more information, see Google Fonts.

Our website may link to our Google Business Profile or other third-party websites. If you follow an external link, that service's privacy practices apply to your use of that service.

Local Service Areas and Location Information

Our website identifies the areas where we provide services, including Allegheny, Lawrence, and Beaver counties in Pennsylvania, to help customers and search engines understand our service coverage. This information describes our service area; it does not mean the website itself asks for your device's precise location. If you provide a property or service address when contacting us, we use it in connection with your service request.

How Information May Be Shared

Information you provide may be handled by communication, hosting, and other service providers involved in delivering the website or responding to your request. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the rights and safety of customers or others.

Retention and Security

Customer communications and service-related information may be kept as needed for scheduling, providing services, maintaining business records, and meeting applicable legal obligations. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. GitHub and other third-party providers manage information under their own policies.

Cookies and Analytics

As currently configured, this website does not include an operator-installed analytics or advertising tracking tool. Third-party services used to deliver website resources may process technical information as described above. If analytics, advertising pixels, embedded maps, or other tracking features are added, this section will be updated.

Your Privacy Questions

To ask about personal information you have provided to JM Heating & AC, or to request a correction or deletion, contact us at jake@jmheating.com or (724) 612-0749. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and our recordkeeping obligations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if our website, services, or information-handling practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
